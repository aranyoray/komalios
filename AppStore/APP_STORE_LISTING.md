## App Description

**Your child's AI-powered shield for the internet.** Komal keeps kids safe online with real-time content filtering powered by on-device machine learning — no data leaves the phone.

The internet is vast and unpredictable. Komal uses five specialized AI models running directly on your child's device to detect and block harmful content before it reaches their eyes. From cyberbullying to mature content, financial scams to horror imagery — Komal catches what traditional blocklists miss.

**WHY PARENTS CHOOSE KOMAL:**

- AI That Learns What Blocklists Can't — Five on-device ML models classify web content in real time, catching threats that static filters miss.
- Privacy-First Design — All content analysis happens on-device. No browsing data is ever sent to external servers for processing.
- Age-Appropriate Thresholds — Filter sensitivity adjusts automatically based on your child's age group (under 10, 10-13, 13-16, 16+).
- Instant Blocking — Harmful content is blocked in under 30ms, faster than a page can fully load.
- Parent Dashboard — Monitor browsing activity, review AI classification reports, and export data as CSV.

**WHAT KOMAL FILTERS:**

- Explicit and mature content
- Cyberbullying and harassment
- Horror and disturbing imagery
- Financial scams and gambling
- Parasocial manipulation
- Short-form content loops (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

**BUILT FOR FAMILIES:**

- Easy onboarding with child profile setup
- Multiple child profiles with individual settings
- Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In
- Riki — a friendly AI companion for check-ins
- Mindful break reminders for healthy screen habits
- Insights dashboard showing browsing patterns

**TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

- 5 CoreML models running in parallel
- Hybrid filtering: AI + curated blocklists
- Works offline — models are embedded in the app
- Lightweight — under 50MB total
- iOS 16+ support

Download Komal today and give your child a safer internet experience.
