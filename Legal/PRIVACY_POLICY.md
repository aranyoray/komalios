# Privacy Policy

**Komal: Learn with AI**
**Effective Date:** February 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** February 15, 2026

Komal ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Komal: Learn with AI mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

**Komal is designed for families with children. We take privacy — especially children's privacy — seriously.**

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- **Authentication credentials** via Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In (name, email address, and a unique user identifier)
- **Child profile information** you provide: child's name (or nickname) and age group

### 1.2 Browsing Data
- **URLs visited** within the Komal safe browser
- **Content classification results** generated by our on-device AI models
- **Browsing session metadata** (timestamps, duration, pages visited)
- **Blocked content logs** (URLs that were blocked and the reason)

### 1.3 App Usage Data
- **Engagement metrics** (time spent in app, features used)
- **Filter preference settings** configured by parents

### 1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do **not** collect location data
- We do **not** collect contacts or address book data
- We do **not** collect photos, videos, or media from the device
- We do **not** use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs for ad targeting
- We do **not** sell, rent, or trade any personal information

---

## 2. How We Process Data

### 2.1 On-Device AI Processing
All content classification and filtering is performed **entirely on the device** using CoreML models. **No web page content, text, or images are transmitted to external servers for AI analysis.** The five machine learning models that classify content (cyberbullying, horror, mature content, financial scams, and parasocial manipulation) run locally on the user's iPhone.

### 2.2 Cloud Sync (Firebase)
We use Google Firebase for:
- **Authentication** (managing sign-in sessions)
- **Firestore Database** (syncing parent settings and child profiles across devices)
- **No Firebase Analytics or Advertising SDKs are enabled**

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Komal is designed for use by families, including children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations:

- **Parental Consent:** A parent or guardian must create the account and set up child profiles. Children cannot create accounts independently.
- **Minimal Data Collection:** We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide safe browsing functionality.
- **No Behavioral Advertising:** We do not serve ads to children or use their data for advertising purposes.
- **No Third-Party Data Sharing:** We do not share children's data with third parties for marketing or advertising.
- **Parental Access and Control:** Parents can view, modify, or delete their child's data at any time through the App's settings.
- **Data Deletion:** Parents can request complete deletion of their child's data by contacting us or using the in-app account deletion feature.

---

## 4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the safe browsing service
- Sync parent settings and child profiles across devices
- Generate AI classification reports for parents
- Improve our content filtering accuracy
- Provide customer support
- Comply with legal obligations

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- **On-Device Storage:** Browsing history and classification results are stored locally using Core Data (encrypted SQLite database on-device).
- **Cloud Storage:** Account and profile data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, which provides encryption at rest and in transit.
- **Data Retention:** Browsing history is retained locally on the device. Parents can clear this data at any time. Cloud-stored account data is retained until the account is deleted.
- **Security Measures:** We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.2+) for all data in transit. Firebase provides SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3 certified infrastructure.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** Google Firebase (authentication and database hosting) processes data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
- **Safety:** We may share information if we believe it is necessary to protect the safety of a child, prevent fraud, or address security issues.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate personal data
- **Delete** your personal data (right to be forgotten)
- **Export** your data in a portable format (CSV export is available in the App)
- **Withdraw consent** at any time by deleting your account

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@komalkids.com or use the in-app account management features.

## 8. GDPR Compliance (European Users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA):
- **Legal Basis:** We process personal data based on (a) your consent, (b) performance of our contract with you, and (c) our legitimate interest in providing a safe browsing experience for children.
- **Data Controller:** Komal is the data controller for personal data processed through the App.
- **Data Transfers:** Data stored in Firebase may be processed in data centers outside the EEA. Google provides Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers.
- **Data Protection Officer:** For GDPR-related inquiries, contact privacy@komalkids.com.
