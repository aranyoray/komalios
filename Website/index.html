Frequently Asked Questions

How does Komal protect my child online?
Komal uses 5 on-device AI models to classify web content in real time. It detects cyberbullying, mature content, horror imagery, financial scams, and parasocial manipulation — blocking harmful pages before they fully load. Combined with curated blocklists, it provides comprehensive protection.
Does Komal send my child's browsing data to the cloud?
No. All content analysis and classification happens entirely on the device using Apple's CoreML framework. No web page content, text, or images are sent to external servers for AI processing. Only account settings and child profiles sync to Firebase for multi-device access.
Is Komal COPPA compliant?
Yes. Komal is designed for families with children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Only parents can create accounts, data collection is minimal, no ads are served to children, and parents can view, modify, or delete all child data at any time.
What age groups does Komal support?
Komal supports four age groups with automatically adjusted filter sensitivity: under 10 (strictest), 10-13, 13-16, and 16+ (most permissive). Parents can further customize filter preferences for each individual child profile.
Can harmful content still get through?
No filtering system is 100% perfect. Komal's AI models are designed to be highly accurate, but false negatives (harmful content not caught) can occur with novel or edge-case content. We continuously update our models and blocklists. Komal is designed as a supplementary tool alongside active parental guidance.
Does Komal work without an internet connection?
The AI classification models are embedded in the app and work offline. However, since the browser requires internet access to load web pages, content filtering during offline browsing applies to cached or locally stored content.
